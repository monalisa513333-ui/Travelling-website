FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a tour package? ▼
Select your desired package, choose your travel dates, number of travelers, and click Book Now. You'll receive a confirmation email within minutes.
What is your cancellation policy? ▼
We offer free cancellation within 24 hours of booking. After that, cancellations made 14+ days before departure receive a 75% refund. Within 7 days: 50% refund.
Do you offer travel insurance? ▼
Yes! We partner with leading insurers to offer comprehensive travel insurance covering medical emergencies, trip cancellation, lost luggage, and more. Add it during checkout.
How far in advance should I book? ▼
We recommend booking at least 2-3 months in advance for peak season travel (June-August, December-January). For off-peak travel, 4-6 weeks is usually sufficient.
Can I modify my booking after confirmation? ▼
Yes, modifications are allowed subject to availability. Contact our support team or log in to your account to request changes. Modification fees may apply.
Do you offer group discounts? ▼
Absolutely! Groups of 10+ travelers receive 15% off. Groups of 20+ receive 20% off. Contact our group travel specialist for custom quotes.
